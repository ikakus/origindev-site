Side Detection config.h v
SPLIT_HAND_PIN: tie the pin HIGH on one half (right), LOW on the other (left) via pull resistor or VCC/GND wiring.
MASTER_LEFT / MASTER_RIGHT: hardcodes which half is the USB master. Use when auto-detect is unreliable. Mutually exclusive with SPLIT_USB_DETECT.
